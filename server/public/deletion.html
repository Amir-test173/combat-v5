World Dominion - Data deletion

World Dominion currently uses guest identities rather than email/password accounts.

Delete room identity in the app

  1. Open the active online room.
  2. Open Settings → Privacy and licences.
  3. Select Delete my data from this room.

This removes the guest player identity from that room and returns controlled territories to AI control. Limited abuse-prevention records may be retained when necessary to protect the service.
