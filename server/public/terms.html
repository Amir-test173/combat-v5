World Dominion Terms & Community Rules

Effective: 24 August 2026

World Dominion is a fictional strategy simulation. Countries, conflicts and outcomes in the game are gameplay systems and are not political or territorial claims.

Community rules

  • Do not use offensive, hateful, sexually explicit or threatening display names.
  • Do not harass other players.
  • Do not exploit vulnerabilities, automate abusive traffic, cheat or attempt to disrupt the service.
  • Do not use the service for unlawful activity.

Moderation

Players can report another player and locally block their displayed name. Reports may be reviewed by the service operator. Access to multiplayer may be restricted for abuse.

Availability

Online service availability is not guaranteed. Game balance, rules and supported countries may change as the game is updated.

Virtual gameplay

Current resources, armies and territory are virtual gameplay data only and have no cash value. The release candidate does not include real-money purchases.

Contact

Use the developer support email shown on the official Google Play listing.
